ACCESS HOSPITAL WEBSITE
=======================

Files:
- index.html
- about.html
- services.html
- departments.html
- appointment.html
- contact.html
- assets/css/styles.css
- assets/js/main.js

How to use:
1. Copy the entire access-hospital folder into XAMPP's htdocs folder.
2. Start Apache in XAMPP.
3. Open http://localhost/access-hospital/

Important before publishing:
- Replace the placeholder telephone and email on contact.html with the hospital's official contacts.
- Connect appointment.html and contact.html forms to PHP/MySQL, SMTP, or your preferred backend.
- Verify the exact departments/services against current hospital operations and licensing.
- Replace/adjust wording, photos, logo and doctor profiles with official hospital information.
