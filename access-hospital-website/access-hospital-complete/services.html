Hospital Services

Comprehensive clinical and supportive services for urgent needs, routine care, diagnosis, treatment and recovery.

Emergency & Urgent Care

Prompt assessment and management for patients needing immediate attention.

Outpatient Consultations

Medical consultations, review visits and referrals to appropriate specialties.

Inpatient Care

Nursing and medical management for admitted patients through recovery.

Maternity

Antenatal, labour and delivery, postnatal and maternal support.

Newborn & Paediatric Care

Clinical services supporting newborns, children and adolescents.

Surgery

Pre-operative assessment, surgical procedures and post-operative care.

Laboratory Services

Laboratory investigations that support diagnosis and treatment decisions.

Radiology & Imaging

Diagnostic imaging services to support clinicians in assessment and follow-up.

Pharmacy

Dispensing and medication support with patient-focused counselling.

Dental Care

Routine oral health assessment, prevention and treatment services.

Eye Care

Eye assessment, screening, treatment and referral support.

Mental & Psychosocial Support

Confidential support, assessment and referral for mental and emotional wellbeing.

Important: The final published service list should be aligned with the hospital's currently licensed departments, clinicians, equipment and service hours.

Clinical support services

  • Health screening and preventive care
  • Chronic disease follow-up and monitoring
  • Minor procedures and wound care
  • Medical referrals and specialist coordination
  • Patient education and health counselling
  • Discharge planning and continuity of care
