About Access Hospital

A patient-focused private hospital built to make quality healthcare more accessible to the community.

Who we are

A hospital built around access, dignity and quality.

Access Hospital is a privately owned hospital located in Namuwongo, Kampala. Our goal is to provide dependable, professional healthcare in a setting where patients and their families feel respected and supported.

Our hospital model brings together outpatient consultations, emergency attention, diagnostic services, inpatient care, maternity, paediatrics, surgery and other supporting clinical services.
